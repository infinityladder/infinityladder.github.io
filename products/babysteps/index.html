Parenting App

Babysteps

A calm log for the first months. Log a feed, a nappy or a pumping session in one tap, see when the next feed is due, and follow your baby's weight on the WHO growth charts. Share the log with your partner through your own Google Drive, so you both see the same day.

Made for tired parents

Fast to log, easy to read at 3am.

One tap to log

Feed, Nappy and Pump sit on the Today screen. The home-screen widgets log a bottle or a nappy without even opening the app.

Today vs usual

See the last feed, when the next one is due, and how today compares with the last few days at the same time of day.

Shared between parents

Turn on sync and invite your partner. The log lives in one file in your own Google Drive, and both phones stay up to date.

Key features

Everything in one simple log.

  • Log breastfeeds, expressed milk and formula, wet and dirty nappies, pumping, weight, temperature and baths
  • Last feed and next feed due, with today's totals at a glance
  • Today vs usual: today's bottle millilitres, feeds or breast minutes against your recent days
  • Weight on the WHO Child Growth Standards, with corrected age for babies born early
  • Optional feed reminders, with quiet hours
  • Temperature readings above your chosen level are highlighted
  • Home-screen widgets to log without opening the app
  • Optional sync with your partner through your own Google Drive
  • Import and export your data as CSV
  • Two calm themes, each light or dark

Babysteps is a log, not medical advice. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any medical condition. If you are worried about your baby's health, talk to your doctor, midwife or child health nurse.
