Bilby

Delete your Bilby data

Bilby (Bilby: Baby Feed Tracker, by Infinity Ladder Technologies) keeps your baby's log on your phone and, if you turn on sync, in one file in your own Google Drive. We don't have a server and don't hold any of your data, so you can delete all of it yourself. You don't need to ask us.

Steps to delete your data

  1. On your phone: to delete one entry, tap the delete button next to it in History. To delete every entry of a type, open Settings → Import / export CSV, go to Clear data and choose feeds & nappies, weights, temperatures, pumpings or baths. To delete everything at once, including your baby's details and settings, uninstall Bilby, or go to Android Settings → Apps → Bilby → Storage → Clear storage.
  2. In Google Drive, if you turned on sync: deleting entries while sync is on also deletes them from the sync file. To delete the file itself, open Google Drive, find "Baby Tracker Sync.json.gz", delete it, then empty Drive's Trash. Signing out of Google in Bilby stops sync but doesn't delete the file.
  3. On other phones: each phone that synced keeps its own copy. Repeat step 1 on those phones. To stop sharing, remove the other person's access to the file in Google Drive.
  4. Exported files: CSV files you exported are not deleted with the app. Delete them wherever you saved or sent them.
  5. Device backup: if Android backup is on, your Google account may hold a backup of the app's data. You can delete it in your Google account's backup settings (Google One, or Android Settings → System → Backup).

What is deleted and what is kept

  • Deleted: everything you delete in the steps above. That includes your baby's details, feeds, nappies, weights, temperatures, pumping sessions, baths, notes and settings, plus the sync file and its contents.
  • Kept by us: nothing. We never receive your data.
  • Retention: Google Drive keeps deleted files in Trash for 30 days unless you empty it. Backups follow your Google account's backup settings.
