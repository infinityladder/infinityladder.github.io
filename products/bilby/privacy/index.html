Privacy

Bilby privacy policy

Bilby keeps your baby's log on your phone. If you turn on sync, it is stored in your own Google Drive. We don't have a server, and we don't receive your data.

Last updated: 2026-11-28

Who we are

Bilby ("the app") is made by Infinity Ladder Technologies, Australia ("we", "us"). This policy applies to the Bilby app for Android.

Data the app handles

To work, the app stores the following on your device:

  • Your baby's profile: name, date of birth, sex, birth weight and gestation at birth, used for age, growth charts and percentiles
  • Feeds and nappies: date and time, breastfeeding minutes on each side, expressed milk and formula amounts, wet and dirty nappies
  • Weights, temperatures, pumping sessions and baths, each with its date and time
  • Notes you add to any entry
  • Settings, such as the theme, feed reminders, quiet hours and the temperature alert level
  • If you turn on sync: the email address of the Google account you connect, and the ID of the sync file

Without sync, this data stays on your device. The app has no account of its own, and we don't run a server.

Data we collect and share

We don't receive any of your data. This version of the app doesn't show ads and contains no analytics or tracking services.

Data leaves your device only in the ways you choose, described below: Google Drive sync, inviting someone to your sync file, exporting a CSV file, and your device backup.

Google Drive sync

Sync is optional and off until you turn it on. When you connect a Google account, Google handles the sign-in. The app asks only for access to the files it creates or you pick in Google Drive, not to the rest of your Drive.

While sync is on, the app keeps a copy of all the data listed above, except your settings and account details, in one file ("Baby Tracker Sync.json.gz") in your Google Drive, and sends your changes to it over an encrypted connection. That file is stored by Google in your Google account, under Google's privacy policy. We can't see it.

When you invite someone, such as a partner, the app shares the sync file with the email address you enter and gives that person edit access. Google emails them a link. They can then see and change the baby's log from their own phone. You can remove their access in Google Drive at any time.

Health information

Your baby's feeds, nappies, weight and temperature are health information. They are stored on your device and, if you turn on sync, in your own Google Drive. We don't use them for advertising, and we don't sell them. The app is a log, not medical advice: growth percentiles use the WHO Child Growth Standards and are for information only.

Export and import

When you export, the app makes CSV files of your entries and opens Android's share menu, so you can send or save them with the app you choose. That app or service handles them under its own privacy policy. When you import, the app reads only the file you pick.

Device backups

If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include the app's data in your device backup and restore it when you set up a new phone. That backup belongs to your Google account and is handled under Google's privacy policy. We can't see it.

Permissions

  • Notifications: feed reminders and the temperature alert
  • Alarms and reminders: so feed reminders go off at the right time
  • Run at startup: to set your reminders again after the phone restarts
  • Internet: only for Google sign-in and Drive sync

Libraries the app is built with also list permissions to schedule background work, keep the phone awake briefly, vibrate, check whether the network is available and use the fingerprint sensor. Android doesn't ask you for these, and the app doesn't use them to read any of your personal data.

How your data is protected

On your device, data is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps can't read. Sync talks only to Google, over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and only someone you have invited can open your sync file.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can edit or delete any entry in the app, and delete all entries of a type on the app's Import / Export screen. To delete everything on the phone, clear the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app.

The sync file stays in Google Drive until you delete it there. Deleting entries in the app while sync is on also deletes them from the sync file. Disconnecting Google in the app stops sync but doesn't delete the file. Files you exported are not deleted with the app.

Children

The app is for parents and carers, and it is not directed at children. The baby's details in it are entered by the parent or carer. We don't knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because we don't hold any of your data, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You control it in the app and in your Google Drive. If you have a question or concern, contact us below. In Australia, you can also contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC). If you are in the EU or UK, you can contact your data protection authority.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app handles data differently, for example by showing ads, we will update this policy before that version is released and change the "Last updated" date above.
