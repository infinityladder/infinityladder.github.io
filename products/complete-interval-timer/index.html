Mobile App

Complete Interval Timer (CITimer)

Interval timing for workouts, study sprints, and rehab. Build any routine with prepare, work, rest, and optional mid-set switch cues. Voice guidance, beeps, and a screen that stays awake keep you focused. Favourites launch fast, light/dark themes match your vibe, and everything stays private on your device.

Key features

Everything you need to build fast, flexible interval sessions.

  • 🎯 Unlimited custom presets with segments, sets, and per-segment timings
  • 🧩 Two editors: quick Basic mode and full Advanced mode
  • ⏱️ Prepare/work/rest controls plus per-segment switch timers
  • 🔊 Voice announcements (names, transitions, countdowns) + toggleable beeps
  • ⭐ Favourites list, quick-start preset, and import/export via JSON
  • 📊 Calendar stats, recent sessions, and total time tracking
  • 🌓 Manual light/dark themes with Material 3 polish
  • 📱 Screen stays on during active timers
  • 🔒 No accounts, no ads, no data collection; everything is stored locally
  • 🔄 Works great for HIIT, Tabata, circuits, boxing rounds, running intervals, yoga flows, stretching, and pomodoro-style focus blocks
