Mobile Game

Glitchoku

Sudoku that gets your heart pounding. The same 9×9 grid and the same pure logic, with a clock that fights back. Every board is generated on your device, has exactly one solution, and can be solved by reasoning alone. Fully offline, with no ads and no sign-up.

Privacy policy ->

Three ways to play

Pick a difficulty, then pick your pressure.

Overclock

One clock covers the whole board. Every correct digit buys time back, and finishing a 3×3 box refills it. Three lives, and each wrong digit costs one.

Blitz

30 seconds per move, and something is chasing you. Mistakes and timeouts let it close in; five correct moves in a row pull you clear.

Classic Sudoku

No limit and no score. Pencil marks, 200 moves of undo, and your own best time to beat. Put a board down for a week and pick it up again.

Key features

Built to be the sudoku game we want on our own phones.

  • Four difficulty tiers set by the hardest technique a board needs, not by counting clues
  • Expert opens in each mode after three finished Hard boards in that mode
  • How to Play teaches every technique on a real board, from naked singles to forcing chains
  • A report after every run: the cells you filled, your closest call, and a move-by-move replay
  • Personal bests and Mission Records for every mode and tier
  • Dark and light themes, high-contrast palettes and reduce-motion support
  • Phones and tablets, in portrait or landscape
  • 100% offline, no ads, no account, and no data collected
