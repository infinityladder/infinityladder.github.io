Simple Body Log

Your weight and body measurements on one body map. Weigh in, or tap a part of the body and slide the ruler to record a measurement. Watch the trend, set a goal, and keep every entry on your own phone. No account needed.

Track your body, not just your weight

Everything you measure, in one place.

Body map

Your latest numbers sit next to the body, male or female. Tap a part, slide the ruler, done. Pick from 16 measurements and name up to 3 of your own.

Trend and goal

Charts for your weight and every measurement. Set a goal weight and see how far you've come since the start date you choose.

Health screen

BMI, waist-to-height and waist-to-hip ratio from your own numbers, with standard or Asian BMI ranges. Pick which ones show.

Key features

A simple log that stays out of your way.

  • Log your weight in seconds, with a main weigh-in window for your daily number
  • 16 body measurements, from neck, chest and waist to biceps, thighs and calves, plus 3 of your own
  • Male or female body map
  • Trend charts for your weight and every measurement
  • Goal weight with progress, days left and weekly rate
  • BMI, waist-to-height and waist-to-hip ratio on one Health screen
  • Browse, edit and add past entries
  • Kg or lb, cm or in; light and dark themes; phones and tablets
  • Import and export your data as CSV
  • No account needed. Your measurements stay on your phone
