Privacy

Simple Body Log privacy policy

Simple Body Log keeps your weight and body measurements on your phone. This version of the app doesn't collect or share any personal data.

Last updated: 2026-11-25

Who we are

Simple Body Log ("the app", shown on your phone as Body Log) is made by Infinity Ladder Technologies, Australia ("we", "us"). This policy applies to the Simple Body Log app for Android.

Data the app handles

To work, the app stores the following on your device:

  • Your weigh-ins: date, time and weight
  • Your body measurements, including any custom measurements you name
  • Your goal weight and the start date you choose
  • Your height, if you enter it, to work out BMI and waist-to-height ratio
  • Settings, such as units, body type, weigh-in window and which health analyses to show

This data stays on your device. We don't receive it: the app has no account and no server, and it doesn't use the internet.

Data we collect and share

We don't collect or share any personal data. This version of the app doesn't send data to us or to anyone else, and it contains no advertising, analytics or tracking services.

Health and fitness information

Your weight and body measurements are health information. They are stored only on your device. We don't use them for advertising, and we don't sell or share them.

Export and import

When you export, the app saves a CSV file with your entries to a folder you pick. When you import, it reads only the file you pick. If you save the file to a folder that syncs to a cloud service, or send it to someone, that service handles it under its own privacy policy.

Device backups

If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include the app's data in your device backup and restore it when you set up a new phone. That backup belongs to your Google account and is handled under Google's privacy policy. We can't see it.

Permissions

The app doesn't ask for any permissions.

How your data is protected

Your data is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps can't read. Nothing is sent over the network.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete or edit any entry in the app. To delete everything, clear the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app. Files you exported are not deleted with the app.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because we don't hold any of your data, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You control all of it in the app. If you have a question or concern, contact us below. In Australia, you can also contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC).

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app handles data differently, for example by showing ads, offering purchases or using the internet, we will update this policy before that version is released and change the "Last updated" date above.
